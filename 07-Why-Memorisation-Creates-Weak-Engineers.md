# 📚 Why Memorisation Creates Weak Engineers

Knowing commands is useful.

Understanding systems is valuable.

---

## Example

Memoriser:

```text
I know the SSH command.
```

Engineer:

```text
I understand why SSH exists.
```

Solutionist:

```text
I understand secure communication.
```

---

# 💡 Solutionist Wisdom

Tools change.

Concepts remain.

## Why Should You Care?

Many people try to learn technology by memorising commands.

This works until something changes.

Real production systems rarely break in exactly the same way as a tutorial.

---

## Simple Explanation

Memorisation teaches you what to type.

Understanding teaches you what to do.

Technology changes constantly.

Principles do not.

---

## Real World Analogy

Imagine learning to drive by memorising:

```text
Turn Wheel Left

Press Brake

Press Accelerator
```

Without understanding road signs, hazards or traffic rules, eventually you'll encounter something unexpected.

The same applies in engineering.

---

## The Difference

### Memoriser

```bash
sudo systemctl restart nginx
```

"I saw this command in a tutorial."

### Solutionist

```text
Users cannot access website

Investigate first

Is Nginx running?

Can server reach network?

What changed?

What do logs show?
```

---

## Production Example

A website goes offline.

Weak engineer:

```text
Restart Everything
```

Solutionist:

```text
Check Service Status
      ↓
Check Logs
      ↓
Collect Evidence
      ↓
Identify Cause
      ↓
Apply Fix
```

---

## Key Takeaways

✅ Commands are tools.

✅ Understanding is more important than memorisation.

✅ Technology changes.

✅ First principles remain valuable forever.

---

## 🧠 Solutionist Thinking

Ask yourself:

- What problem am I solving?
- Why does this command exist?
- What would happen if it failed?

Don't focus on buttons.

Focus on systems.
