# 🕵️ Think Like A Detective

Every production issue leaves clues.

---

## Detective Questions

Who reported it?

What changed?

When did it start?

What systems are affected?

Can the problem be reproduced?

---

## Server Example

Website unavailable.

Possible causes:

- DNS
- Application
- Database
- Networking
- Deployment

Investigate before acting.

## Why Should You Care?

Every problem leaves evidence.

Every outage leaves clues.

The answer is usually hidden somewhere in the evidence.

---

## Simple Explanation

Good engineers investigate.

Bad engineers guess.

Troubleshooting is often detective work.

The goal is to follow the clues.

---

## Real World Analogy

A detective arrives at a crime scene.

They do not immediately accuse someone.

They gather evidence.

```text
Observe
    ↓
Collect Clues
    ↓
Build Theory
    ↓
Verify Evidence
```

Engineers use the same process.

---

## The Five Solutionist Questions

Before touching anything ask:

### 1. What is broken?

### 2. What should happen?

### 3. What is happening instead?

### 4. What changed?

### 5. What evidence exists?

---

## Production Example

Website unavailable.

Bad Response:

```text
Restart Servers
```

Good Response:

```text
Can website be reached?

Can server be reached?

Are services running?

What changed recently?

What do logs show?
```

---

## Key Takeaways

✅ Evidence beats assumptions.

✅ Investigation beats guessing.

✅ Every problem leaves clues.

✅ Questions reveal solutions.

Act like a detective.
