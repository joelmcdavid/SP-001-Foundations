# The Art Of Asking Better Questions

## Why Should You Care?

The quality of your questions determines the quality of your solutions.

Weak questions produce weak answers.

---

## Simple Explanation

Experienced engineers ask better questions.

They focus on facts and evidence.

---

## Poor Questions

```text
It doesn't work.
```

```text
The server is broken.
```

```text
Everything is down.
```

---

## Better Questions

```text
What exactly is failing?

When did it begin?

What changed?

Can it be reproduced?

What evidence supports this?
```

---

## Real World Analogy

A doctor doesn't ask:

```text
Are you sick?
```

A doctor asks:

```text
Where does it hurt?

How long has it hurt?

When did it start?

What triggers it?
```

Engineers should think similarly.

---

## Production Example

Bad Report:

```text
Website Broken
```

Good Report:

```text
Website returns HTTP 500 errors.

Issue started after deployment.

Only affects checkout service.

Error visible in logs.
```

---

## Key Takeaways

✅ Details matter.

✅ Questions reveal evidence.

✅ Better questions reduce guesswork.

✅ Communication is an engineering skill.

---

## 🧠 Solutionist Thinking

The difference between average and exceptional engineers is often the quality of their questions.
