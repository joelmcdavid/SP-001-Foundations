# The Solutionist Framework

## Why Should You Care?

Every lesson in this academy leads to a single framework.

A structured way to solve problems.

---

## Simple Explanation

The Solutionist Framework is a repeatable process for approaching technical challenges.

---

## Step 1 - Observe

```text
What is happening?
```

Gather facts.

---

## Step 2 - Investigate

```text
Collect Evidence
```

Logs.

Metrics.

Alerts.

Reports.

---

## Step 3 - Hypothesise

```text
What might be causing it?
```

Create possible explanations.

---

## Step 4 - Test

```text
Prove Or Disprove
```

Use evidence.

Avoid assumptions.

---

## Step 5 - Solve

```text
Apply Fix
```

Resolve the root cause.

---

## Step 6 - Document

```text
Record Lessons Learned
```

Help future engineers.

---

## Step 7 - Teach

```text
Share Knowledge
```

Create more Solutionists.

---

## Production Example

```text
Observe
    ↓
Investigate
    ↓
Hypothesise
    ↓
Test
    ↓
Solve
    ↓
Document
    ↓
Teach
```

---

## Key Takeaways

✅ Every problem has evidence.

✅ Evidence drives investigation.

✅ Investigation drives solutions.

✅ Knowledge should be shared.

---

## 🧠 Solutionist Thinking

A technician fixes problems.

An engineer prevents problems.

A Solutionist solves problems and teaches others to solve them too.

> We don't create button-clickers.
>
